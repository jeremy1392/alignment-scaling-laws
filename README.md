# AI Safety Fun: four games on alignment scaling

By [Jeremy Canale](https://www.jeremycanale.com). Play online at **[www.aisafety.fun](https://www.aisafety.fun)**.

Four small browser games on one question: does the cost of keeping an AI model safe grow faster or slower than
the model? Open `index.html` (the hub) in a recent browser and pick a game, then an angle:

- `replay.html`, *The experiment*: a replay of what was measured on real models (start here);
- `money.html`, *The company*: the safety bill against the revenue of an AI company;
- `garden.html`, *The garden*: weeding hours against the size of a garden, with hidden roots;
- `house.html`, *The house*: the toy model of the preprint, simplified (flies, traps, an inspection).

The four angles (`?world=`) are three hypothetical laws, `helps` (α = 0.7), `balance` (α = 1), `debt` (α = 1.5),
and `measured`, whose exponents come from preregistered studies (osf.io/wda8q, osf.io/q2j3y) through
`js/measured.js`, generated by the analysis code of the preprint. The shared rules are in `js/law.js`.
Add `?fast` to play four times faster. Old links `index.html?world=…` open the house game.

The games accompany the preprint *Toward Alignment Scaling Laws: A Framework and First Preregistered
Measurements* (Jeremy Canale, 2026), whose sources, code and data are distributed with its arXiv submission.
They teach the framework; they are not evidence.

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, no build step (jQuery from a CDN). Code under the MIT license (`LICENSE`).
